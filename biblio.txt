#### temporality in large language models
#Temporality in large language models (LLMs) refers to their ability to understand, reason about, and process temporal information, such as the sequence, continuity, and progression of events over time. Research highlights several aspects:
#
#    Temporal Reasoning: LLMs often struggle with tasks requiring temporal understanding, such as predicting event sequences or reasoning about time-sensitive information. Benchmarking studies reveal gaps in their temporal comprehension and reasoning capabilities, though fine-tuning can improve performance 
#
#Temporal Blind Spots: LLMs face challenges due to the temporal limitations of their training data, which may lack freshness or comprehensive historical coverage. This affects their ability to handle both past and recent temporal queries effectively
#
#Time Directionality: Studies show asymmetry in LLMs' ability to predict forward versus backward in time, which is theoretically surprising but explained by computational and sparsity considerations
